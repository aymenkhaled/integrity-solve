### F. Escalations & SMR Workflow (5 min)

> "This is where most platforms stop. We continue to the end — all the way through to SMR submission."

**Demonstrate the full SMR workflow:**

1. **Create escalation** — attach customer, summary of grounds, risk rating
2. **Escalate to SMR** — adds reason, moves status
3. **Draft SMR** — narrative text, suspicious acts list, subject details (aligned to AUSTRAC's SMR form)
4. **Approve** — dual-authorisation step (COMPLIANCE_OFFICER or WORKSPACE_ADMIN must approve)
5. **Submit** — records submission timestamp, reference, locks draft

> "Australian reporting entities must file SMRs within three business days of forming suspicion under s.41 of the AML/CTF Act. This workflow enforces that. The submitted SMR is stored permanently in the audit log."

**Key legal points to name-drop:**
- s.41 suspicious matter reports
- s.43 threshold transaction reports (coming in next release)
- AUSTRAC's Financial Intelligence Portal (FIP) integration on the roadmap
