Thanks for your interest in the Integrity Solve AML/CTF portal development opportunity.
At this stage, we are speaking with a number of potential developers and technical partners to assess the most commercially sensible pathway to get a credible proof of concept live without committing to a large upfront custom build before the model has been validated with early users.
The portal concept is not intended to be a conventional software project where we simply commission a full SaaS build at significant upfront cost. We are assessing a staged model that may involve:
a lean proof of concept;
use of an existing platform such as Assembly where it reduces build time and cost;
targeted custom development only where genuinely required;
clear visibility over client payments, provider costs, developer costs, and retained margin;
an ongoing commercial model linked to chargeable checks, completed workflows, maintenance, and platform performance.
The commercial structure we are considering is broadly:
a modest initial goodwill/project payment, potentially around USD $1,000, payable once an agreed proof of concept has been completed and demonstrated;
a clearly defined proof-of-concept scope focused on the minimum viable workflow;
an ongoing payment model based on chargeable checks or completed paid workflows processed through the portal;
clear exclusions for failed checks, internal testing, retries, refunds, or provider errors;
ongoing developer responsibility for maintaining, supporting, and improving the parts of the portal they build.
We are open to practical advice on the best technical pathway. However, proposals that require a significant upfront commitment to a full custom build before proof of concept will not be considered at this stage.
The priority for Integrity Solve is commercial alignment. We are looking for a developer or technical partner who is prepared to help validate the model, move quickly, keep the initial build lean, and participate in the upside if the portal performs.
If that type of structure is of interest, please provide a short response setting out:
whether you would consider this staged commercial model;
whether you would recommend Assembly, another platform, or a lightweight custom build for proof of concept;
what you would include in the first proof-of-concept scope;
what you would need from us to confirm feasibility.
